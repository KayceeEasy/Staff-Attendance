# 📍 Staff Attendance Portal

A simple, smart, and secure digital system for tracking daily employee attendance.

---

## 📱 What is this project?

The **Staff Attendance Portal** is a web application designed for businesses to manage staff check-ins effortlessly. Employees can quickly sign in and sign out of work from their mobile phones or computers, while managers get real-time tracking, attendance history, and automated reports.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- **📍 Office Location Check**: Staff can only sign in when they are physically at the office. The app checks their GPS location to ensure they are on-site before accepting a check-in.
- **🔒 Anti-Cheating Device Lock**: Prevents employees from signing in for absent colleagues. Each staff account is securely locked to that employee's specific phone or device.
- **📡 Works Without Internet**: If Wi-Fi or mobile network drops, staff can still check in. The app saves their attendance record safely and automatically sends it as soon as connection is restored.
- **📅 Hybrid & Remote Work Tracker**: Seamlessly tracks office days alongside Work-From-Home (WFH) schedules using clear color codes:
  - 🟩 **Signed In**: Checked in on time at the office.
  - 🟨 **Late**: Checked in past the company's start time.
  - 🟦 **Home**: Scheduled working from home for the day.
  - 🟥 **Absent**: No check-in recorded on an office day.
- **📊 Manager Dashboard**: Gives administrators full oversight of daily attendance, staff list management, work schedule settings, and one-click data downloads.

---

## 👤 How It Works for Staff

1. **Open the App**: Launch the app on your phone or web browser.
2. **Select Your Name**: Choose your name from the staff dropdown list.
3. **Check In / Out**: Tap **SIGN IN** when arriving at the office, or **SIGN OUT** when leaving for the day.

---

## 🛡️ How It Works for Managers

- **Live Attendance Feed**: Instantly view who has arrived and what time they checked in.
- **Weekly Attendance Matrix**: Get an at-a-glance weekly summary of every staff member's attendance status.
- **Device Lock Management**: Easily unlock an employee's account if they change or replace their phone.
- **Exportable Reports**: Download attendance data into spreadsheet formats (CSV) for HR and payroll processing.
