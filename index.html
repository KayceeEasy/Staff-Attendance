      Privacy & Location Policy

      The Perimetrr Workspace Platform is engineered with zero-surveillance architecture to guarantee individual privacy while maintaining authentic workplace presence verification.

      📍 Instantaneous Proximity Verification (Zero Tracking)

      GPS coordinates are sampled strictly at the exact instant you tap "Sign In" or "Sign Out" to calculate your distance from the authorized office perimeter. Your route history, travel speed, and personal off-duty locations are never monitored, recorded, or transmitted.

      🔔 Passive Perimeter Arrival Reminders

      Mobile arrival prompts rely on your phone OS's native low-power perimeter listener. The app stays dormant until your hardware enters the workplace radius to fire a single local notification. Zero location data leaves your device.

      🔐 Hardware-Level Biometric Protection

      Touch ID, Face ID, and Device PIN authentication are processed exclusively within your phone's hardware Secure Enclave via W3C WebAuthn standards. Biometric traits remain locked inside your device and are never uploaded or accessible to any server.

      🏢 Multi-Tenant Data Isolation & Employee Rights

      Attendance logs (timestamp, verification method, perimeter status) are encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256), isolated within your employer's workspace vault. You maintain rights to access and verify your attendance records via HR.
