Step 1 of 5 Platform Quick Guide

Workspace & Staff Pairing

Employees don't need app stores. Simply share your unique 6-character pairing code or QR code. When staff open the link on their phone or browser, their device instantly pairs to your workspace.

Find your code and QR anytime in the Account Settings tab or header.

Office Geofence & Work Hours

Calibrate your office GPS with 1-tap in System Configuration. Set your office radius (e.g. 100m), morning late cutoff time, and closing time (e.g. 5:00 PM).

Post-Closing Sign-Out allows staff who verified in at the office to sign out remotely if they forget before leaving.

Staff Roster & Biometric Locks

Add employees individually or bulk-import via CSV in the Staff tab. The system locks each employee to their first authorized device, completely preventing buddy punching.

Employees can also enable Face ID / Fingerprint biometrics on their device for 1-tap sign-ins.

Hybrid Weekly Matrix

Manage flexible work arrangements directly from the Dashboard Matrix or Hybrid Portal. Toggle any employee day between Office and Home.

On scheduled Home days, staff can sign in remotely without triggering geofence distance violations.

Live Feed, Quotas & Exports

Track real-time check-ins under Logs and review overall attendance percentages in Analytics. You can configure whether Home days count toward your attendance quota.

Click Export CSV or print formatted PDF summaries anytime for monthly payroll processing.
